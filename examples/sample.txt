Staff Data Engineer — Northwind Logistics
Location: Manchester (hybrid, 2 days/week in office)

About the role

Northwind moves freight for 400+ retail customers across the UK and EU.
Our data platform is the backbone of pricing, capacity planning and the
customer-facing tracking portal — and it hasn't kept pace with the
business. We're hiring a Staff Data Engineer to lead the rebuild: set the
technical direction, bring the rest of the data team (5 engineers) with
you, and own delivery end to end.

What you'll do

- Define and drive the migration from our nightly batch pipeline to a
  streaming-first architecture, with clear milestones the exec team can
  track.
- Own data quality and reliability for the pricing engine, where a bad
  number costs us real money and customer trust.
- Set standards for the team — testing, code review, on-call — and coach
  engineers toward them rather than gatekeeping.
- Partner with Product and Commercial to turn vague asks into a roadmap.

What we're looking for

- 8+ years in data engineering, with at least 2 leading a platform
  rebuild or major migration that actually shipped.
- Deep experience with a stream processing framework (Kafka, Flink,
  Spark Structured Streaming) in production, not just a POC.
- Fluency in SQL and Python, and strong opinions about data modelling.
- A track record of raising the bar on a team without becoming a
  bottleneck.
- Comfort operating with ambiguity and limited direction — you'll often
  be the most senior data person in the room.

Nice to have

- Logistics, supply chain or marketplace background.
- Experience with dbt and a modern warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery).
- Exposure to cost control on cloud data infrastructure.

We move quickly and expect the people we hire to do the same. This is a
role for someone who wants ownership, not a ticket queue.
